# SamePart Knowledge Base

Research and analysis behind our entry for **SIH26099 — AI-Driven Standardization and
Harmonization of Material Codes Across CPSEs** (Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas /
Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited).

These notes exist so that nobody on the team re-researches something we already settled,
and so the claims we put on a slide are ones we can defend when a judge pushes back.

already been tried, reversed, and why.

## Conventions used in these notes

- **Verified** means a primary source was reached and read.
- **Vendor-published** means a company selling the product is the only source. Usable, but
  say so out loud if we cite it.
- **Unverified** means we could not reach the source. It is not the same as false.

Every numeric claim here carries its provenance. If a number has no source next to it,
treat it as folklore and do not put it on a slide.
